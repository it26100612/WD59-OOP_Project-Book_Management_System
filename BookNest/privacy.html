YOUR PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Learn how BookNest handles your information when you use our online bookstore.

Last updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to BookNest. We value the privacy of our customers and aim to handle personal information responsibly. This policy explains the types of information that may be collected when you use our website and how that information may be used.

2. Information We Collect

When you create an account, browse books or place an order, the website may collect the following information:

  • Account information: Name, email address and account details.
  • Contact information: Phone number and delivery address provided at checkout.
  • Order information: Books ordered, quantities, order total and order status.
  • Website activity: Information needed to support basic website features such as the shopping cart and account session.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information provided to BookNest may be used to:

  • Manage customer accounts.
  • Process and manage book orders.
  • Arrange delivery to the address provided.
  • Display order history and order status.
  • Respond to customer questions and support requests.
  • Maintain and improve website functionality.

4. Account and Order Information

Account and order information helps the website associate orders with the relevant customer. Customers should keep their login details private and log out when using a shared device.

Please ensure that the contact and delivery details you provide are accurate.

5. Payment Information

Payment options and processing depend on the functionality enabled on the website. Do not submit full card details or other sensitive payment information through an ordinary message or an unapproved form.

Refer to the payment method shown during checkout for the applicable payment instructions.

6. Information Protection

BookNest aims to handle customer information responsibly. However, no website or electronic storage method can guarantee absolute security.

Access to customer information should be limited to authorized purposes. Users should protect their passwords and avoid sharing account credentials.

7. Sharing of Information

Customer information should only be shared when necessary to provide a requested service, comply with applicable legal requirements or address legitimate security concerns.

BookNest should not disclose personal information for unrelated purposes without an appropriate basis.

8. Cookies and Local Storage

Depending on the website implementation, browser storage may be used to remember information such as cart contents, session details or website preferences.

Clearing browser data may remove locally stored website information. The exact behaviour depends on how the website is configured.

9. Your Responsibilities

  • Provide accurate account and delivery details.
  • Keep your login credentials confidential.
  • Notify support if you notice suspicious account activity.
  • Use the website responsibly.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when website features or information-handling practices change. The latest version should be published on this page.

Questions About Privacy?

If you have questions about this policy or your information, contact the BookNest support team.
